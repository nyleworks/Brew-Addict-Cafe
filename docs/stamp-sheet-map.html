Stamp card · Google Sheet

Brew Addict stamp sheet

The Google Sheet behind the stamp card has three tabs. The Apps Script writes to them for you, so nobody types in the sheet. This page shows what each tab holds and how a stamp gets there. The rows below are made-up examples.

How a stamp reaches the sheet

Customer's phone

Stamp card on the website

The phone makes a random 22-character code once and shows it as a QR. No name or phone number.

Cafe phone

Staff page

Staff scan the QR with the camera. Signed in to the cafe Google account, they tap Add stamp or Give free drink.

Read only: "how many stamps?"↓
Add stamp / Give free drink↓
Apps Script

Code.gs, bound to the sheet

Turns the card code into a one-way fingerprint, checks the rules (one stamp a day, 10 for a free drink, card ends a month after its first stamp), then writes. A nightly clean-up runs at 4am Kundasang time.

Row 2 is part way through a card. Row 3 (highlighted) just got its 10th stamp and is waiting for a free drink. Row 4 got a free drink this morning, so it reset to 0 and its free drink count went up. Row 5 ran out of time with 3 stamps and was cleared by the nightly clean-up.

  • AcardA one-way fingerprint of the customer's code (32 characters). The real code never reaches the sheet, so a copy of the sheet can't be used to stamp cards.
  • BstampsStamps on the current card, 0 to 10.
  • CcardStartWhen the first stamp of this card was added. The card ends one month after this. Empty when the card is at 0.
  • DlastStampDateDay of the last stamp, in Kundasang time. Used for "one stamp a day".
  • EfreeDrinksHow many free drinks this card has had, ever.
  • FcreatedAtWhen this row was first made (UTC).
  • GlastActivityLast stamp or free drink (UTC). A card with nothing here for about 6 months is deleted.

  • AtimeDate and time of the event, in Kundasang time.
  • BdateJust the day, as text.
  • CcardFirst 12 characters of the card fingerprint, enough to match it to the Cards tab.
  • Deventstamp, free_drink or expired.
  • EstampNumberFor a stamp: which stamp it was (10 means the card is full). For expired: how many stamps were lost.
  • FweekdayMon to Sun. Feeds "Stamps by day of the week".
  • Ghour0 to 23. Feeds "Stamps by hour".
  • HdrinkFree drinks only: the drink staff picked, if they picked one.
  • IdaysToFillFree drinks only: days from the first stamp to the free drink.

What each action writes

Add stampCards: stamps +1, lastStampDate = today, lastActivity = now (cardStart set on the first stamp). Log: one stamp row.
Give free drinkOnly at 10 stamps. Cards: stamps back to 0, cardStart cleared, freeDrinks +1. Log: one free_drink row with the drink and days to fill.
Nightly 4amUnfinished cards past their month go back to 0 and log an expired row. Cards idle about 6 months are deleted from Cards.
Customer checks cardReads the Cards row only. Writes nothing.

Please don't type in the Cards or Log tabs. A typo in the card column would disconnect that customer's stamps. If something needs fixing, ask Claude.
